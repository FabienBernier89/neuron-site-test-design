Frequently asked questions about pricing

How do I get an offer?

Through a scoping discussion, then a demo on your documents. Your offer is then built on your volumes, your languages and your review level.

Is there a self-service free trial?

There is a trial, but it is supervised and it follows the demo: a scope, users and a duration defined together. For a first look, you can try the live Text and document translation demo without signing up.

Which languages do you work in?

Four native languages, the ones in which Switzerland writes its law: German, French, Italian and English. Thirty languages are available on the platform.

Does my data stay in Switzerland?

Your projects and files are stored in Switzerland with Infomaniak, whichever engine you choose. With LexMachina or in Highly sensitive content mode, processing also stays in Switzerland.

Are the API and on-premises deployment part of the offer?

They are scoped separately: this is an integration project in its own right, defined with your IT department (architecture, sizing, updates and operation) before any commitment.
