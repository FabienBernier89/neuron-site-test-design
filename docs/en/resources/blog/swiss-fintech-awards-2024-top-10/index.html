Neur.on in the Top 10 Swiss fintech start-ups of the Swiss FinTech Awards 2024

Neur.on team19 April 20241 min read
In brief

Neur.on is one of the ten Swiss fintech start-ups selected for the Swiss FinTech Awards 2024, in the ‘Early Stage Start-up of the Year’ category. A panel of 20 experts reviewed more than 60 applications, out of 100 submissions, to select five companies per category. Neur.on will therefore take part in a fintech workshop and the Swiss FinTech Awards Night.

We are proud to announce that Neur.on is in the Top 10 Swiss fintech start-ups of the prestigious Swiss FinTech Awards 2024. Competing in the ‘Early Stage Start-up of the Year’ category, we see this recognition as an acknowledgement of our innovation and our potential in a rapidly evolving fintech landscape.

The selection process was rigorous: a panel of 20 industry experts meticulously reviewed more than 60 applications, out of a total of 100 submissions. Their task: to identify the five most promising companies in each of the ‘Early Stage Start-up of the Year’ and ‘Growth Stage Start-up of the Year’ categories.

As part of this nomination, we look forward to taking part in the leading fintech workshop and the Swiss FinTech Awards Night. These events offer opportunities for networking, learning and showcasing our technology to industry leaders and potential investors.

More news will follow as we continue our journey towards innovation and excellence in fintech.

To find out more, read the Swiss FinTech Awards 2024 announcement.
