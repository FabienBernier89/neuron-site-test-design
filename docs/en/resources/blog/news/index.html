22 October 2024Company

Information security: Neur.on achieves ISO 27001 certification

What do you think is the main concern of LegalTech and FinTech companies when they work with new suppliers? In our experience, it is information security.

After all, in these sectors, handling client data according to protocol is not a gimmicky extra. It is absolutely essential.

And while our smiling faces may not show it, we take this subject very seriously. So seriously that we have achieved ISO 27001 certification.

ISO 27001 is an international standard for implementing, maintaining and improving ISMS (information security management systems).

Our processes for accessing, storing and protecting information have been thoroughly checked by an official third-party body.

And as you can imagine, we will not stop there: this T-shirt is only the first in our security fashionista’s wardrobe. More will be printed soon. Because IT security is already woven into everything we do.
