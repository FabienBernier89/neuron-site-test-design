This language pair in practice

Most Swiss cantons administer justice in German. A written submission, general terms and conditions or a ruling drafted in French must therefore also exist in German. That version uses the terms of the federal acts: the ones a German-speaking reader recognises and the ones the court will cite.

The word already exists

Every concept in the Code of Obligations has an official German term in the version published alongside the French text. The translator does not choose between several synonyms: they use that term. Both versions can be read side by side in Fast lookup CHnell, each with its reference.

What gets converted

COOR

Abbreviations to convert

CO becomes OR, CC becomes ZGB, CP becomes StGB, LP becomes SchKG, LDIP becomes IPRG. The Banking Act becomes BankG, not a guessed abbreviation of Bankengesetz.

ßss

Never ß

Swiss Standard German always writes ss: Strasse, Abschluss, gemäss. A text delivered with ß shows at once that it was not written for Switzerland. The rule also applies to headers, tables and file names.

15'000

Amounts and dates

Thousands keep the apostrophe (15'000), and amounts stay in Swiss francs. Format conversions break financial tables more often than vocabulary errors do.

forGerichtsstand

False friends from French-speaking Switzerland

The ‘for’ of a contract (its place of jurisdiction) is a Gerichtsstand, not merely a place (Ort). An administrative appeal is not a Rekurs in every procedure. These slips come from the French of France or from cantonal usage.

What Corrext guarantees for this pair

Two native languages

French and German are two of the four native languages of LexMachina, trained on more than 15 million Swiss legal and financial segments. Together with Italian and English, they are also the only languages available in Highly sensitive content mode.

Consistency over time

The same term must be rendered the same way in the contract, its annexes and the amendments signed six months later. CHnell's terminology resources carry these choices from one project to the next.

Compounds to rebuild

Where French lines up four words, German expects a single compound: obligation de réparer le dommage becomes Schadenersatzpflicht. A literal word-for-word rendering reads like a translation.

Three moments when a German version is expected

Litigation

A French-language submission, filed in German

The prayers for relief and the case citations are converted first: ATF becomes BGE, CO becomes OR. The legal review then focuses on the argument, which must read like a text written in German.

Companies

General terms and conditions for the whole of Switzerland

A jurisdiction clause, a prescription clause and a contractual penalty clause leave no room for interpretation: they use the terms of the Obligationenrecht. Consistency between the two versions is checked line by line.

Authorities

A press release for two language regions

The German text must appear on the same day, in the same register. Translation projects organises the internal review and calculates the time needed from the number of standard pages detected.

Frequently asked questions about French to German translation

Should the text be in Standard German or Swiss German?

In Standard German, in its Swiss variety. Swiss German is a spoken language: no legal text is drafted in dialect. Swiss Standard German uses ss instead of ß, an apostrophe in thousands, guillemets as quotation marks and the vocabulary of the federal acts.

How do you avoid terms from German or Austrian law?

By starting from Swiss texts rather than a general-purpose corpus. LexMachina is trained on Swiss law, and any doubtful term can be checked in Fast lookup CHnell, which shows the official segment, its source language and its reference. A term that appears in no Swiss text has no place in the document.

Can both language versions of a contract be authoritative?

The parties can agree on that, and it is common for a bilingual Swiss contract. It requires a term-by-term check of both versions, because a difference in wording becomes a difference in law. Double review & certified translation is the review level designed for this case.

Can we require our company's own terminology?

Yes. Fast lookup CHnell covers 30 domains drawn from official sources, and your own resources can be added, with private or shared access. Your in-house terms then appear in later translations, with their context.

Where is the document processed?

Storage is in Switzerland in every case. With LexMachina or in Highly sensitive content mode, processing is too, and the content is not reused as translation memory. Third-party engines, hosted abroad, trigger the Attorney-Client privilege notice, which reminds users of your organisation's policy: the document must be pseudonymised.
