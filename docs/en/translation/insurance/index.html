The acts insurers cite every day

Swiss private insurance rests on two complementary acts: one governs the contract between insurer and insured, the other the supervision of insurance companies. The Code of Obligations and data protection law come into play every day.

VVG

Federal Act on Contracts of Insurance

FRLCAITLCA

It governs the proposal, the policy, the premium, the duty of disclosure and the insured event

ISA

Insurance Supervision Act

DEVAG

It makes insurers and reinsurers subject to a licence and to supervision

CO

Code of Obligations (CO)

DEORFR · ITCO

It applies on a supplementary basis to the insurance contract, for everything the Federal Act on Contracts of Insurance does not regulate

FADP

Federal Act on Data Protection

DEDSGFR · ITLPD

It governs the processing of personal data, including health data, in claim files

Translating insurance documents, in practice

Private insurance translation deals with documents in which every word determines cover: general conditions, proposal, policy, claim notification, settlement correspondence. The core concepts are defined by the Federal Act on Contracts of Insurance, published in German, French and Italian. A gap between two wordings can shift who bears an obligation.

Insurance documents

  1. General conditions

    General insurance conditions are republished at every revision. With the translation memory, only what has changed is retranslated.

  2. Policy and endorsements

    The policy uses the terms of the general conditions. The concordancer lets you check that a word has not drifted from one version to another.

  3. Claim notification

    Notifications arrive in the language of the insured, often handwritten or scanned. The PDF to Word tab first produces an editable document.

  4. Expert reports

    A technical or medical report is translated in its original format, up to 25 files of 50 MB per upload.

  5. Settlement correspondence

    A letter declining or accepting a claim binds the insurer. A Full review by a legal translator checks the scope of every sentence.

Three stages of the insurance cycle that require translation

Underwriting

The general conditions

The general conditions are revised, then republished in German, French and Italian. The translation memory limits the work to the amended clauses, and a Full review covers the passages that determine cover.

Claim

A notification in another language

The notification and its annexes, often scanned, are converted into editable documents and then translated. The claims handler understands the file immediately, without waiting for an external translation.

Settlement

A decision to send to the insured

The letter declining or accepting the claim binds the insurer and may be produced in court. It goes through a Full review by a legal translator, with the delivery time known before you approve the quote.

Frequently asked questions on insurance translation

Do general conditions have to exist in several languages?

An insurer active in several language regions publishes them in the languages of its insured. The difficulty is not publication but consistency: the same word must cover the same thing in every version. The translation memory and the concordancer help you maintain this over time.

How should health data in a claim file be handled?

Storage is in Switzerland in every case. For sensitive data, Highly sensitive content mode requires processing in Switzerland only, with no reuse of the content in a translation memory. Before anything is sent, engines hosted abroad display the Attorney-Client privilege notice, which reminds users of your organisation's policy.

Can scanned claim notifications be processed?

Yes. The PDF to Word tab in Text and document translation converts a scanned PDF into an editable Word document, which can then be translated and annotated. This helps with handwritten notifications and with reports sent as images. The resulting file then follows the same workflow as other documents.

What level of review for a letter declining a claim?

A Full review by a legal translator at the very least, because the wording can be held against the insurer. For internal communication or an acknowledgement of receipt, a Light review is enough. You choose the level document by document, with the delivery time shown before you approve.

Does the concordancer cover insurance vocabulary?

Yes, with a Private Insurance domain, and an Employment & Social Ins. domain for files involving social insurance. Each occurrence opens in context with its official source, so you can decide between two plausible wordings. Your own terminology resources are added on top.

How many documents can I upload for a single claim?

Up to 25 files of 50 MB per upload in Text and document translation, in .pdf, .doc, .docx, .xls, .xlsx, .ppt and .pptx formats. For a heavier file or human review, use Translation projects. Documents come back in their original format, annexes included.
