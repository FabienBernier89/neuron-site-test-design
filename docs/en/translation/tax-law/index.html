Tax vocabulary in four languages

Swiss tax vocabulary is misleading from one language to another. The Swiss withholding tax (Verrechnungssteuer) is not an ordinary deduction at source, and taxable profit is not the same as accounting profit. The equivalents above are those of the acts themselves. The acts a tax file cites every day

Swiss taxation layers a federal act, a harmonisation act that frames the cantons, and the cantons' own tax acts. Indirect taxes fall under separate acts, all published in the three official languages.

DFTA

Federal Act on Direct Federal Taxation

DEDBGFR · ITLIFD

It defines tax liability, taxable income, taxable profit and the assessment procedure

StHG

Federal Act on the Harmonisation of Direct Taxes of the Cantons and Communes

DEStHGFRLHID

It imposes a common set of concepts and procedures on the cantons

VAT Act

Federal Act on Value Added Tax

DEMWSTGFRLTVA

It governs VAT liability, tax rates and VAT accounting

VStG

Federal Act on Withholding Tax

DEVStG

It organises the tax deducted at source on investment income, and its refund

Translating a tax file, in practice

Swiss tax translation means working with concepts defined at three levels: direct federal tax, harmonised cantonal taxes and indirect taxes such as VAT. Each act is published in German, French and Italian. In these acts, an everyday word such as income or profit takes on a precise definition, and the amount due depends on it.

Documents in a tax file

  1. Tax assessment

    An assessment arrives in the official language of the canton. The translation helps you decide whether to file an objection, often within a short time limit.

  2. Objection and appeal

    The submission filed binds the taxpayer. It goes through a Full review by a legal translator, with the delivery time shown before you approve.

  3. International context

    Documents exchanged with a foreign tax administration often circulate in English, while the reference remains the German, French or Italian text.

Frequently asked questions on tax translation

In which language do Swiss tax authorities communicate?

In the official language of the canton concerned, which produces multilingual files as soon as a company has establishments in several regions. The platform's four working languages, German, French, Italian and English, are native in every pair.

Is a translation enough to respond to a tax assessment?

Translation helps you understand and decide. The submission filed, whether an objection or an appeal, binds the taxpayer and justifies a Full review by a legal translator. When an attestation is required, Double review & certified translation remains available. The review delivery time is shown before you approve the quote.

How do I translate tax concepts that have no equivalent?

Start from the act rather than from usage. Withholding tax (Verrechnungssteuer), for example, has an official name in each of the three languages and a definition of its own. The Tax & Customs domain of the concordancer shows these occurrences in context with their source.

Are tax files processed in Switzerland?

Storage is on Swiss servers in every case, and processing stays in Switzerland with LexMachina or in Highly sensitive content mode. A file containing taxpayer data should not go to an engine hosted abroad without prior pseudonymisation.

Can I translate the accounting annexes of a tax return?

Yes. The .xls and .xlsx formats are supported like Word, PowerPoint and PDF, up to 25 files of 50 MB per upload. The files come back in their original format with their table structure. For a larger batch, use Translation projects.

What about a simple letter to the tax authorities?

Text translation, in Text and document translation, accepts up to 10,000 pasted characters, with a choice of engine and up to four alternative wordings. It is the right tool for a short letter that must go out within the day. You can then order a Light review if the letter binds the company.
