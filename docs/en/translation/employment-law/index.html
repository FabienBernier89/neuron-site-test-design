The acts an employment contract cites every day

Swiss employment law is split between a contractual part, in the Code of Obligations, and a regulatory part, in the Employment Act. Personnel data and permits fall under yet other acts.

Translating an HR document, in practice

Swiss employment law translation covers documents that frame a lasting relationship: individual employment contract, staff regulations, amendments, termination, reference. The concepts come from the Code of Obligations and the Employment Act, published in German, French and Italian. The wording chosen can decide whether a notice of termination is valid.

HR documents

  1. Staff regulations

    Staff regulations apply at every site. Their translation must be identical in Zurich, Lausanne and Lugano, revision after revision.

  2. Termination letter

    The wording of a notice of termination can be challenged in court. A Full review by a legal translator checks it before it is sent.

  3. Non-compete clause

    The prohibition of competition has a precise legal definition. Its translation cannot be left to everyday usage.

  4. Foreign employees

    Documents relating to work permits fall under another act, the Foreign Nationals and Integration Act, with its own vocabulary.

Frequently asked questions on employment law translation

Does an employment contract have to be in the employee's language?

The law does not require a particular language for the individual employment contract. But an employer who wants the commitment understood and enforceable has every interest in providing a version in the employee's working language. The platform's four languages (German, French, Italian and English) cover this need.

How do I translate a termination letter safely?

Distinguish the three regimes of the Code of Obligations: ordinary termination, wrongful termination and termination with immediate effect for good cause. The official terms exist in all three languages, and the letter itself justifies a Full review by a legal translator before it is sent.

Can personnel files go through the platform?

Yes, with the right mode. Storage is in Switzerland in every case, and Highly sensitive content mode requires processing in Switzerland only, with no reuse of the content in a translation memory. Before anything is sent, engines hosted abroad display the Attorney-Client privilege notice, which reminds users of your organisation's policy.

How do I keep staff regulations identical at every site?

With the translation memory, which limits the work to the passages changed in a revision. And with terminology resources that belong to your organisation, which Fast lookup CHnell shows on top of its 30 sourced domains. Each term opens in context with its source before you fix it.

Are collective employment contracts covered?

Their vocabulary falls within the Employment & Social Ins. domain of the concordancer, with official sources. Each term opens in context with its reference, so you can check a concept before using it in a contract or in staff regulations.

Can I quickly translate a simple internal memo?

Yes. Text translation, in Text and document translation, accepts up to 10,000 pasted characters, with a choice of engine and up to four alternative wordings. For internal communication, a Light review is usually enough. For a document handed to an employee that might be produced later, a Full review is the safer choice.
