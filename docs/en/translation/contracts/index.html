The acts a contract cites every day

The Code of Obligations holds most of the Swiss vocabulary of contracts. But a contract cites other acts as soon as it touches on ownership, non-payment or a party based abroad.

CO

Code of Obligations (CO)

DEORFR · ITCO

It governs the formation, performance and extinction of contracts, as well as company law

CC

Swiss Civil Code

DEZGBFR · ITCC

It sets out the concepts of persons, ownership and good faith, which contracts refer to without redefining them

DEBA

Federal Act on Debt Enforcement and Bankruptcy

DESchKGFRLP

It governs what follows a payment default, from the summons for payment (Zahlungsbefehl) to bankruptcy

PILA

Federal Act on Private International Law (PILA)

DEIPRGFR · ITLDIP

It determines the applicable law and the place of jurisdiction as soon as a party or a performance is located abroad

Translating a contract, in practice

Translating a contract under Swiss law means carrying obligations that have legal effect into another language. Each term refers to a concept defined by the Code of Obligations, published in German, French and Italian. The translation keeps the wording of the official text and leaves article references, time limits and amounts untouched.

Contracts and their schedules

  1. Lease and annexes

    Upload the lease, the handover report and any addenda in File translation. Each document comes back in its original format, with references to the CO intact. Formats .pdf .doc .docx .xls .xlsx .ppt .pptx, up to 25 files of 50 MB per upload.

  2. General Terms and Conditions

    General terms and conditions are rewritten block by block from one version to the next. In Standard confidentiality, the project feeds a translation memory that only your organisation can reuse.

  3. Framework agreement and amendments

    An amendment must use the exact vocabulary of the original contract. The term chosen the first time is one highlight away in Fast lookup CHnell.

  4. Clauses with figures

    Interest rates, notice periods for termination and amounts bind the parties. In a Full review, a legal translator checks them line by line. The delivery time is shown before you approve the quote.

Three situations where contract translation gets complicated

Commercial lease

Zurich landlord, French-speaking tenant

The lease is drafted in German, the property manager replies in French, and both parties cite the same articles of the CO. The translation uses the official wording of each concept rather than an approximate equivalent, and the document comes back in its original layout.

Financing

A loan agreement to sign tomorrow morning

The machine translation goes out in the evening with LexMachina, the Full review is ordered straight away and the delivery time is shown before you approve. The borrower receives a text reviewed by a legal translator, not a draft to correct.

International

Swiss law, negotiated in English

The parties' English has no official version: each concept must be tied to the German, French or Italian term of the CO. CHnell shows the original article, the Alternatives panel shows up to four competing wordings, and the legal expert decides.

Frequently asked questions on contract translation

Why not translate a contract with a general-purpose translation tool?

The Swiss vocabulary of contracts is fixed by legislation published in three languages. A general-purpose tool offers a plausible translation, not the wording of the Code of Obligations. Then there is the question of where the data is processed. With Corrext, storage is in Switzerland in every case, and processing stays in Switzerland with LexMachina or in Highly sensitive content mode.

Is the layout of the contract preserved?

Yes. The document comes back in its original format, with its articles, paragraphs and annexes. The application recommends uploading the native file, Word or Excel, rather than a PDF when you have it. For a scanned contract, the PDF to Word tab first produces an editable document.

Who reviews a contract before it is signed?

A legal translator: in a Light review for a consistency check, or in a Full review for a line-by-line check. For a document that binds the company towards a third party, Double review & certified translation involves two translators and a specialised agency, with a certificate. You choose the level for each document.

Are my contracts used to train an engine?

No. Client content is not used to train the engines. Projects in Standard confidentiality can feed a translation memory that only your organisation can reuse. Projects in Highly sensitive content mode are never reused, not even internally.

How do I keep the same term from one amendment to the next?

Check the term chosen the first time. Highlight a word and Fast lookup CHnell opens it in context, with its official source. The concordancer also accepts your own terminology resources, on top of the 30 domains it already covers. Projects in Standard confidentiality also feed a translation memory that only your organisation can reuse.

Can I translate an excerpt from a contract without uploading a file?

Yes. Text translation, in Text and document translation, accepts up to 10,000 characters pasted directly, with a choice of engine and up to four alternative wordings. It is the right tool for a single clause under negotiation. You copy the result straight into the document you are drafting.
