How to name us

Four names, four distinct roles. Confusing them is the most common mistake: Neur.on is not the name of the platform, and Corrext is not the name of the engine. This section is also intended for search engines and for the assistants that cite our pages.

Neur.on

refers to the company and to the solution as a whole. Full company name: Neur.on AI Solutions SA.

is written with a full stop between ‘Neur’ and ‘on’, with no space and only an initial capital. Never ‘Neuron’, never ‘NEUR.ON’.

Corrext

refers to the platform, the working environment in which users translate, review and manage their projects.

is written as one word, with an initial capital, no accent and no hyphen.

LexMachina

refers to the engine, that is, the neural machine translation engine trained on Swiss legal and financial data. The name is reserved for the engine.

is written as one word, with a capital L and a capital M.

CHnell

refers to the terminology concordancer: searching for a term in its official Swiss context.

‘CH’ in capitals, ‘nell’ in lower case, as one word, with no space.
